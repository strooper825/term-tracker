# 0022. A homepage for several sections, a map of every seat, and words the owner edits

Date: 2026-11-12
Status: Accepted

## Context

The site was the Congress dashboards and nothing else: `/` redirected to `/members`, the header
was a flat row of four links (two of them to GitHub files), and every word on it lived in a
component. The site is to grow an Elections section, articles and more under the Term Tracker
name, and the owner wants to change the site's own words (homepage text, About, Methodology,
articles) without code.

The homepage design settled with the owner (mockup, 2026-11-12):

- one permanent header on every page: Support, the wordmark, search; then Home, Congress (menu),
  Elections (soon), Articles, About (menu);
- search for members and for bills;
- a national map of real state and district shapes, House and Senate views, colored by the party
  holding each seat (independents purple, a split Senate delegation striped), hover to see who
  holds a seat and click through to them, zoom and drag with no scroll bars;
- latest articles; a dark footer with the mission and quick links. No "last updated" on the
  homepage, no GitHub or data-dictionary links in the chrome.

The map has to cover all 535 seats, but the mart only holds the 20 tracked members.

## Decision

- **Who holds each seat** is a new mart model, `mart.seat`, from the latest congress-legislators
  snapshot, for every member, with `is_tracked`. `GET /api/v1/congress/seats` serves it. A tracked
  member links to their dashboard; anyone else to their official website (Bioguide when the
  source has none) until coverage reaches all 535.
- **Shapes** come from `python -m ingest.national_map`, which projects the Census 1:20,000,000
  state and district files of the vintage the nightly uses (`census_geography_year`) into one
  drawing with Alaska and Hawaii as insets, and writes `site/public/maps/national.json` (about
  230 KB, committed). Shapes change only with new district lines, so the file is regenerated by
  hand then; colors come from the build's seat data every night. The territories' delegates are
  listed under the map rather than drawn.
- **Search** runs in the browser over `/search/members.json` and `/search/bills.json`, written by
  static route handlers at build time and fetched only when someone types.
- **The owner's words** are files under `site/content`: `site.json` (homepage text, footer
  mission, Ko-fi address, contact email, social links), `pages/*.md` (About, Methodology,
  Privacy) and `articles/*.md`. `/admin` is Sveltia CMS, a static page that edits those files
  through GitHub with the owner's own token; a push to `main` touching only `site/content` or
  `site/public/uploads` triggers `deploy.yml`.
- **Party colors** keep their meaning and gain one: Independent is purple (#6B3FA0) on badges,
  filter chips, the composition bar and the map. Vote bars keep their own Yea/Nay scheme.

## Consequences

- Every page uses `PageShell` (header, sheet, footer); a new section is a nav entry and pages.
- `mart.seat` is built from every current legislator, so the nightly dbt build covers 540 rows
  more than the tracked set; the homepage is one more page needing the API at build time.
- After a new Congress convenes, congress-legislators records new House terms over several days
  (ADR 0021), so seats may show as vacant until it does.
- District shapes are the 119th Congress's until the Census publishes the 120th's; states that
  redrew for 2027 will be out of date on the map until then, and the map says so.
- The Support page links nowhere until `kofiUrl` is set; Contact and the social row appear only
  once set.
