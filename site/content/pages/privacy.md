title: Privacy policy
description: What Term collects about visitors (very little) and why.
---

Term has no accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising, and it sets no cookies of its own.

## What is collected

The site is served by Vercel, which keeps standard web server logs (such as the address a request came from, the page requested and the browser used) to operate and protect the service. Term does not use those logs to identify anyone.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to the public records they cite and to members' official websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Support payments

If you choose to support the site through Ko-fi, that payment happens on Ko-fi under Ko-fi's own privacy policy. Term sees only what Ko-fi shares with the page owner.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
