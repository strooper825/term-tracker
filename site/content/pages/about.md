title: About Term Tracker
description: Who runs Term Tracker, what it covers, and how it stays nonpartisan.
---

Term Tracker follows members of Congress through their terms: how they vote, what they sponsor, which committees they sit on, how their campaigns are funded, and what their offices say in public.

Everything on the site comes from public records, is refreshed every night, and links back to the record it came from. Nothing is editorialized: the site shows what the records say and leaves the judgement to you.

## What is covered

Today the site has full term dashboards for a set of members of the House and Senate, an overview of the current Congress, and a page for every bill those members have sponsored or acted on. The map on the homepage covers every seat in Congress. Coverage of every member, and an elections section, are on the way.

## Nonpartisan and independent

Term Tracker is not affiliated with any party, campaign, candidate or advocacy group. Party colors on the site only ever mean party: red for Republican, blue for Democratic, purple for independent.
